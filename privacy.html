Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

At ManRose, we respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you visit our website.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the ManRose website located at https://barry-09.github.io/manrose-website/ and explains the information practices applicable to the website.

This website is currently designed primarily to provide information about the ManRose social application and its features.

2. Information We Collect

The ManRose website does not currently require visitors to create an account, maintain a user profile, publish content, send messages, or use other social features directly through the website.

We do not currently operate a website-based registration or contact form that requires visitors to submit personal information.

3. Technical Information

When you access a website, the hosting infrastructure used to deliver the website may process basic technical information required to provide, maintain, and secure the service.

Such information may include technical request data, browser or device information, approximate network information, and access timestamps. The availability and handling of such information may depend on the hosting provider's own systems and policies.

4. How Information Is Used

Information associated with visits to the website may be used as necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website and to identify or troubleshoot technical issues.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The ManRose website does not currently use Google Analytics or intentionally configured third-party advertising or tracking services.

If these practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe the relevant practices.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to third-party services, including Google Play and other external websites.

When you follow a third-party link, your interaction with that service is governed by the third party's own privacy policy and terms. ManRose does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. ManRose Mobile Application

The ManRose mobile application is separate from this website and may provide features such as accounts, profiles, posts, reels, stories, messaging, following, discovery, and competition features.

Information collected or processed through the mobile application is subject to the privacy practices applicable to the application and may differ from the practices described in this website Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled in connection with the website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

ManRose does not currently maintain a website-based user account system or database for visitors to this website.

Technical information handled by the hosting infrastructure may be retained according to the applicable policies and operational requirements of the hosting provider.

10. Children's Privacy

The website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Because the website does not currently provide account registration or a website-based personal data management system, there are limited privacy controls available directly through the website.

If you contact us regarding a privacy matter, we will review the request and respond where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its functionality, or its information practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
